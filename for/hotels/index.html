How it works

Everything below is live today — not a roadmap.

Room bookings, no phone tag

Guests pick their dates and room type right on your site. No back-and-forth, no double-booking the same room to two different guests.

Deposits that protect your rooms

Turn on deposits for any room type — a guest secures their stay with a small upfront payment via secure EcoCash. Fewer no-shows on your busiest weekends.

WhatsApp confirmations, sent for you

Guests get a booking confirmation the moment they reserve, and a reminder before check-in — automatically, no front desk effort required.

Special requests, handled properly

Late check-in, extra bed, dietary needs — guests can note it when booking, and you approve or decline before it's confirmed. No surprises at the door.

Common questions

What if I don't want to take deposits?

Totally fine — deposits are optional per room type. Turn them on only where no-shows actually cost you.

Can guests book multiple nights, or just single dates?

Multi-night stays — guests pick a date range, and pricing calculates automatically for the length of stay.

What if EcoCash isn't a guest's preferred payment method?

More payment options are on the way. For now, EcoCash covers the majority of Zimbabwean and regional guests.
