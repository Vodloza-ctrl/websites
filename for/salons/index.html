How it works

Book anytime, no phone tag

Clients pick a service and time slot right on your site. No calls, no back-and-forth. You see every booking the moment it's made.

Deposits that protect your time

No. Booking happens on your website, reminders arrive on WhatsApp — nothing to install.

More payment options are on the way. For now, EcoCash covers the majority of Zimbabwean clients.
